    Welcome to Better-Brain

    AI assistants forget everything between chats — you re-explain who you are and what you're building every time. Better-Brain fixes that.

    It creates a small folder of Markdown notes — a second brain you own — and connects your AI tools so they read and write it automatically. Set it up once; every assistant remembers.

    This wizard will:

    • Create your brain vault, in a layout that fits how you work
    • Connect your assistants — Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Windsurf, Cline
    • Wire up integrations you choose — GitHub, Obsidian, optional add-ons
