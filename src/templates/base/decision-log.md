# Decision log

Dated decisions and the reasoning behind them — so a future session (or future you)
doesn't re-litigate a settled call or repeat a dead end. Newest at the top.

**Format:**

```
## YYYY-MM-DD — short title
**Decision:** what was decided.
**Why:** the reasoning.
**Status:** active | superseded by <link>.
```
